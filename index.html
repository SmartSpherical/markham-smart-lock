Questions常见问题

Will it fit my door?我的门能装吗？

Most solid wood, fibreglass and steel entry doors can be converted. That's why we check your door first — if it isn't a good fit, we'll tell you and you pay nothing.大多数实木、玻璃钢及钢制入户门都可以改装。所以我们会先免费上门查看——如果不适合，我们会如实告知，您无需支付任何费用。

What happens if the batteries run low?电池没电怎么办？

The lock runs on batteries and warns you before they run out. You can always open the door with the backup mechanical key. We'll show you how to change the batteries during setup.门锁使用电池供电，电量不足前会提醒。随时可用备用机械钥匙开门。安装时我们会教您如何更换电池。

Can I install it myself?可以自己安装吗？

Only if your door is already prepared for this style of lock. Most doors in Canada need to be cut and converted, so we recommend the installed package.只有门已经预留这种锁的开孔才可以。加拿大大多数门需要开槽改装，因此建议选择含安装套餐。

Do you install in condos?公寓可以安装吗？

Condo apartment doors are usually fire-rated and controlled by building rules, so we focus on houses and townhouses. Ask us and we'll tell you honestly.公寓单元门通常为防火门，且受物业规定限制，因此我们主要服务独立屋和镇屋。欢迎咨询，我们会如实告知。

What does the warranty cover?保修包括什么？

For one year we'll repair or replace the lock if it stops working properly, and fix any installation problem, at no charge. We're local, so you can reach us directly.一年内如门锁无法正常使用，我们免费维修或更换，安装问题也免费处理。我们就在本地，随时可以联系。

Which areas do you serve?服务哪些地区？

All of Markham, including Unionville, Cornell, Berczy, Cathedraltown and Milliken. Richmond Hill, Vaughan and Scarborough on request.万锦全区，包括 Unionville、Cornell、Berczy、Cathedraltown 及 Milliken。列治文山、旺市及士嘉堡可预约。
